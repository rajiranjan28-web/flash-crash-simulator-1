What happens when the market crashes?

MochaTrade lets people trade US stocks and crypto with borrowed money. See what a crash does to traders and to us, with and without our fixes.

A simple model with made-up traders. It compares choices. It is not MochaTrade's real data.

Leverage.Borrowing to bet bigger. The more you borrow, the smaller the fall that wipes you out.
Wiped out.The app force-sells your bet because losses got too big. The technical name is liquidation.
Glitch.A wrong, lower price on our app. Traders get sold at a price that is not real.

Our refund rule

Real crash: no refundIf the whole market fell, that is trading risk. We explain it and do not repay it.
Glitch: refundSold at a wrong price, or when the real price never hit your limit? We repay the loss the glitch caused.
Capped at ₹1 croreAll refunds come from one fund. If it runs empty, we say so publicly and refunds stop.

How the model works
  • 5,000 traders. Typical deposit ₹10,000. Three in four bet the price goes up. They borrow 2 to 50 times their money.
  • One volatile US stock. The real price falls, then bounces back a little, or not at all on the worst day.
  • A trader is wiped out when losses eat almost the whole deposit.
  • The glitch: few buyers on our app, so its price dips below the real one. Forced selling pushes it lower, wiping out more traders. The chain reaction fades after a few minutes.
  • "Lost to the glitch" is the extra loss from being sold more than 1% below the real price, plus the whole loss of anyone who would have survived at the real price.
  • Not included: fees, taxes, several stocks at once, and law. It compares choices. It does not predict exact rupee amounts.
